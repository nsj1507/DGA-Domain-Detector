We need to fix the current DGA Domain Detector based on a specific failure observed during our project review.

The professor tested short/random-looking DGA-like domains and several were classified as Legitimate.

Our current dataset has a strong domain-length imbalance: legitimate domains contain many short domains, while DGA domains are disproportionately longer. Therefore the ML model may have learned domain length as a shortcut.

DO NOT simply add a hardcoded rule such as "short domain = DGA" or "short domain = Legitimate."

The model must learn the distinction from real training data.
